Logo Clear Space

Room to Breathe

The minimum clear space around the wordmark is defined as 1N, the cap-height of the 'N' letterform. No text, graphic, or edge may enter this zone.

1N 1N 1N 1N

What is 1N?

The N unit equals the cap-height of the NU letterform in the logo. At the most common display sizes:

  • → Logo at 200px wide: 1N ≈ 22px
  • → Logo at 30mm (print min): 1N ≈ 3.5mm
  • → Logo at 120px (digital min): 1N ≈ 13px

Minimum Sizes

Full Wordmark

120px wide (digital) · 30mm wide (print)

24×24px (digital) · 8mm (print)

Visual Texture

The Nuvexa brand pattern is derived from the NU› logomark and the gold chevron motif. It serves as a proprietary visual texture for backgrounds, packaging, stationery, and environmental applications. Never use as a primary focal element — it supports, never dominates.

05  /  Brand Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

The Nuvexa brand earns trust through consistency. Every application of the identity must respect these rules without exception.

Logo Usage

✓  Do

Use the Navy Blue wordmark on Campus Cream backgrounds. This is the primary logo combination and must be used in all formal contexts.

✗  Don't

Never place the logo on Gold, Red, Navy or any unapproved background color. Always use Cream as the logo background.

Voice & Tone

"3 campuses. 194 days total.
All delivered on schedule."

✓  Do

Write with specific numbers. Use concrete outcomes. Let the data carry the credibility. Short sentences. No adjectives that can't be measured.

"We are passionate leaders
driving holistic ecosystems."

✗  Don't

Never use vague buzzwords: passionate, holistic, ecosystem, synergy, visionary. They signal the exact consultant-speak Nuvexa is positioned against.

If we miss your deadline,
we take full accountability, in writing.

✓  Do

Lead with accountability. Reference the ironclad guarantee often. It is the single most differentiating claim Nuvexa has, own it confidently.

Permanent Logo Rules

  • → Never stretch or distort the wordmark
  • → Never add drop shadows or glow effects
  • → Never recolor the mark in Gold, Terracotta, or Teal — approved only in Navy and Cream
  • → Never place on photographic backgrounds without a solid color block underneath
  • → Never use obsolete logo versions, always use current approved files
  • → Minimum digital size: 120px wide / Print: 30mm wide

Color Usage Rules

  • → Navy + Cream is the primary color pair, always lead with it
  • → Gold is a secondary accent, use sparingly for premium signals
  • → Builder Orange-Red (#F24E24) is reserved for CTAs and urgency only — Terracotta (#894029) is for blueprint/structural accents only
  • → Never pair Terracotta and Gold on the same component
  • → Never use the palette colors at reduced opacity as tints, use the utility grays instead
  • → All body text must meet WCAG AA contrast on its background

C-01  /  Co-Branding

Partner Identity Rules

When Nuvexa appears alongside partner, client, or parent brand marks, specific rules govern the spatial and visual relationship to protect brand integrity for all parties.

Lockup Hierarchy

Nuvexa always appears on the left or top. The partner brand follows to the right or below, separated by a 1px vertical rule in Steel.

NUVEXA
PARTNER

Size & Rules

Nuvexa must never appear smaller than the partner logo. Ideal ratio: 1:1 visual weight.

  • → Minimum 2N clearspace from divider rule to each logo
  • → Remove “A Division of SGB Group” line from co-branded lockups
  • → Nuvexa must be legible at the chosen placement size

Approved Contexts

  • → Joint venture proposals with institutional partners
  • → Campus handover documents alongside client mark
  • → SGB Group internal communications
  • → Award submissions and press releases

Forbidden

  • → Never smaller or subordinate to a vendor mark
  • → No co-branding with unverified partner brands
  • → Never modify Nuvexa mark to match a partner style
  • → No consumer advertising co-branding without approval

C-04  /  Photography & Imagery

Visual Language

Photography is the proof of the promise. Every image communicates transformation, precision, and human impact — not stock-photo polish.

✓  Approved Style

  • → Real campuses, real transformations. Before/after comparisons, construction progress, completed spaces with natural light.
  • → Human-centered: students in completed environments, leaders reviewing plans, site walk-throughs.
  • → Cool highlights, slight de-saturation. Preserve Navy and Cream tones in architecture.
  • → Wide-angle structural shots showing scale and precision craftsmanship.

✗  Prohibited

  • → Generic stock: smiling office people, handshakes, laptops on tables, abstract blue glows.
  • → Heavily filtered images with dramatic saturation or Instagram-style grading.
  • → Logo directly on photography — always use a solid color overlay first.
  • → Third-party campus imagery without verified permission.

Logo on Photography

The logo may never sit directly on a photographic background. Use one of the approved approaches below:

✓ Solid Color Block

Navy or Cream solid rectangle over the photo. Min opacity: 90%.

✓ Gradient Overlay

Navy gradient from bottom (100%) to transparent. Logo placed in solid zone only.

C-06  /  Motion Principles

Movement Language

Nuvexa animations must feel precise and purposeful. Every transition should reflect the forward-momentum character of the brand.

Easing

cubic-bezier(0.25, 0, 0, 1)

Custom ease-out: elements “lock into place.” Reflects construction accuracy.

Duration Scale

  • → Micro-interactions: 150ms
  • → UI transitions: 250ms
  • → Page animations: 400ms
  • → Hero entrances: 600ms

Direction

Elements enter bottom-to-top or left-to-right. Mirrors the upward arrow in the NU› mark.

Entrance: translateY(24px) → 0 + fade. Exit: fade only.

Approved Motion

  • → Fade-in + upward drift for content reveals
  • → Counter animations for metric displays
  • → Progress bar fills for timeline displays
  • → Color transitions on hover (150ms)

Forbidden

  • → Bounce, spring, or elastic easing
  • → Parallax scroll beyond subtle depth
  • → Looping background animations
  • → Any UI animation exceeding 700ms
