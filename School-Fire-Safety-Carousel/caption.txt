When it comes to school fires, history has given us lessons we cannot afford to ignore. 

The most devastating tragedies in educational institutions often trace back to the same preventable architectural and administrative failures. Here is what we must learn:

1️⃣ Lucknow Coaching Centre (2026): High occupancy + limited egress = disaster. Converting commercial buildings for dense educational use without updating escape routes traps occupants. Evacuation planning and strict occupancy limits are non-negotiable.

2️⃣ Chitrakoota Kaushalya School (2026): Top-floor assembly areas are the hardest for emergency services to access. Auditoriums and upper levels require robust active suppression (sprinklers) and early smoke detection.

3️⃣ Har Mandir Boarding School (2025): Most school fires start invisibly. Overloaded or aging electrical infrastructure can smolder for hours. Networked fire alarms and routine electrical audits are lifesavers.

4️⃣ Kumbakonam School (2004): Fire-compliant materials should never be compromised. Combustible roofs and narrow exits accelerate tragedies. Safe construction standards are the absolute baseline.

The safest schools are planned before an emergency ever happens. Safety isn't an add-on; it must be engineered into every campus blueprint from day one.
